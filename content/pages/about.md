## Warm-up

Hey, welcome to my little website. I've been really struggling to code it since I am not quite
the frontend developer, however I really had fun doing it, and you know what? I might continue
maintaining it 'cause it's been a very interesting journey overall.

As you might observe, I am a talkative individual so when you see me, don't hesitate to say
`Hi!`. I'll respond in 90% of the cases, because the rest 10% I'll have my headphones on and
won't typically hear anything.

I have a lot of interests but usually the things associated with me are `Hard-Worker`, `Gym` and
`Engineering`.

## What I actually do

I'm a backend engineer who ended up specializing in the unglamorous middle of the stack:
`distributed systems`, `integrations`, and `data infrastructure`. Most days that means Go or
Python, a queue or a pipeline somewhere, and at least one third-party API doing something it
isn't supposed to. I like that part more than I probably should.

Most of my professional work lives under NDA, which is why this site leans on
[selected case studies](/work) and [writing](/writing) instead of a client list — the problems,
constraints, and tradeoffs are real, the names mostly aren't.

Outside of work I spend time on `Linux`, `infrastructure`, and generally the kind of debugging
that starts with "that shouldn't be possible" and ends a few hours later with a one-line fix and
a new appreciation for whatever system just humbled me. I studied Software Engineering at the
Technical University of Moldova, and I still learn most of what sticks by building something and
breaking it first.

I'm based in Chișinău, Moldova.

Thanks for reading this improvised text, and if you want to contact me, in the header you can
find the link to my LinkedIn.
